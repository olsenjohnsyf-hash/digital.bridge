Digital literacy practice quiz

Answer ten multiple-choice questions about computers, information, online safety, and responsible technology use. Check each answer, read the explanation, then see your score. You can try again. The quiz does not ask who you are.

Need a refresher first? Read Digital Literacy and Online Safety. The answer key lives in js/quiz.js because this project has no server. The goal is practice, not a secret exam.
